Who We Are

Connecting Latin America to Dublin's Educational Ecosystem

Beyond Borders Match is a proposed digital platform connecting adults from Mexico and Brazil with personalised English language and cultural experiences in Dublin, Ireland. Studying abroad involves more than choosing a course: prospective students must also consider accommodation, cost, immigration requirements, cultural adjustment, and opportunities to build a community.

Inspired by modern matching applications, our platform guides users through an interactive questionnaire to match them with suitable schools, neighborhoods, host family accommodation, and local community activities.

Our Mission

To help Mexican and Brazilian adults experience life in Ireland through personalised English language education, meaningful cultural immersion, and supportive local connections.

Our Vision

To become a trusted digital platform for international experiences that helps people find a place where they can learn, grow, and feel a sense of belonging.

Terms & Conditions

Beyond Borders Match operates as a digital intermediary connecting customers with education and experience providers. Partners deliver their own services under agreements. The platform clarifies service providers, payment responsibilities, refunds, complaints, and commissions.

Free and VIP plan conditions are clearly outlined before purchase. A customer's overall trip budget is not company revenue because much of it is paid directly to third-party providers.

Data Protection & Privacy

As an Ireland-based business, Beyond Borders Match complies with applicable GDPR requirements. We collect only the data needed for defined purposes and obtain consent where required.

We limit employee access, use strong passwords and encryption, and establish clear retention periods. Customers can request data access, correction, or deletion at any time.
